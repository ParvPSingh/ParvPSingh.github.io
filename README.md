## Projects

### Research

**Model Merging of Equivariant MLIP Models** _(Current)_
- Extending our GFFMerge work on merging GNN force fields (invariant and direct-force) to state-of-the-art equivariant MLIP models, with a systematic proof of how the technique handles them

**Analysis of Weight-Space Symmetries of Equivariant, Invariant and Direct-Force MLIP Models** _(Current)_
- Investigating hidden, undiscovered symmetries in the weight spaces of MLIP models
- Connecting weight-space symmetries to representation-space symmetries, with symmetry-aware analysis and comparison across MLIP model types

**GFFMerge: Efficient Merging of Graph Neural Force Fields and Beyond** _(ICML 2026)_
- Inspired by model merging in vision and language, introduced the first principled framework for closed-form model merging in GNNs
- Exploited the linear structure of message-passing layers to formulate merging as a convex embedding-alignment problem with an analytical solution
